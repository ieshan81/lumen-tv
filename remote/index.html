PROJECTOR PICTURE

Make it feel right.

Set up your projector picture

  1. Turn on the projector, select HDMI and let its picture settle.
  2. Darken the room, turn off the camera flash, and use a neutral picture preset. Keep Windows Night Light and automatic picture adjustments off for consistent results.
  3. Point your phone at the projected image. Keep it steady and outside the projector beam.
This is guided picture tuning. A phone camera can estimate a tint, but it cannot certify color accuracy. Every adjustment has an Original comparison and a timed rollback.
Photograph the whole chart with all four numbered crosses visible. Keep the phone close to straight-on, lower exposure if white patches look blown out, and use a neutral projector picture preset.
